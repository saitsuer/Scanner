# Privacy Policy for Scanner

**Effective date:** July 26, 2026

Scanner (“the App”) is provided by **[DEVELOPER OR COMPANY NAME]** (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how Scanner accesses, uses, stores, and shares information.

## 1. Information Scanner accesses

### Camera
Scanner requests camera access so you can capture driver’s licenses, state IDs, passports, letters, forms, receipts, and other documents that you choose to scan.

### Files and storage
Scanner may save PDF files and scan images on your device. The App may also open the Android file picker or share sheet when you choose to save, open, export, or share a file.

## 2. Data collection

For the initial ad-free version described by this policy, Scanner is designed to process document images on your device and does not require an account.

Scanner does not sell personal information.

**Important developer verification:** Before publishing, confirm that the production build does not include analytics, crash reporting, advertising, cloud storage, or other third-party SDKs that transmit data off the device. If any such service is included, this policy and the Google Play Data safety form must be updated before release.

## 3. User-initiated sharing

Scanner does not send a scanned document to another person or service unless you choose to save, export, print, or share it. When you share a file through another app or service, that third party’s privacy policy applies to its handling of the file.

## 4. Data retention and deletion

Scans and PDFs stored by Scanner remain on your device until you delete them through the App, your device’s file manager, or by uninstalling the App, depending on where the files were saved.

Files that you exported, copied, printed, backed up, or shared may remain in other apps, devices, or services. Scanner cannot delete copies controlled by those third parties.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable technical measures designed to limit access to data handled by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 6. Children’s privacy

Scanner is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Advertising and future services

The initial version covered by this policy does not include advertising. If advertising, analytics, cloud services, or other data-processing services are added in a future release, this Privacy Policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that release is published.

## 8. Government affiliation
