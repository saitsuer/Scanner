FULL DESCRIPTION
Scanner makes it easy to scan US driver's licenses, state IDs, passports, and multi-page documents, then save them as clean PDF or JPEG files. You can also design a two-sided business card from scratch.

SCAN IDS AND PASSPORTS
Capture the front and back of a driver's license or state ID, or the photo page (and optional visa page) of a passport. Scanner places IDs life-size on one US Letter page, choose stacked or side-by-side placement. Passport pages print at real TD3 size.

SCAN MULTI-PAGE DOCUMENTS
Create one PDF from letters, forms, receipts, and other paper documents. Add multiple pages and keep recent scans organized in one library.

PER-PAGE FILTERS
Choose Color, Enhance, Grayscale, or Black & White for each page as you crop it.

DESIGN A BUSINESS CARD
Fill in your name, title, contact details, and services, pick a brand color pair, add a logo and a QR code, then export a print-ready two-sided card (US standard 3.5x2in) as PDF or PNG. Saved designs can be reopened and edited later.

EXTRACT TEXT (OCR)
Pull text out of any scanned page and copy it to the clipboard.

EDIT SAVED SCANS
Reopen an already-saved document or ID scan to re-crop or change the filter on individual pages, without starting over.

KEY FEATURES
- Driver's license, state ID, and passport scanning
- Life-size ID/passport placement on one US Letter page
- Stacked and side-by-side ID layouts
- Multi-page document PDFs
- Automatic document edge detection with manual crop and rotate
- Color, Enhance, Grayscale, and Black & White filters
- Business card designer with logo, QR code, and brand colors
- PDF and JPEG/PNG export
- On-device OCR text extraction
- Re-edit already-saved scans
- Rename, delete, and share files

Scanner processes everything on your device. Camera access is used only to capture the documents you choose to scan.

Scanner is not affiliated with any government agency, department of motor vehicles, or licensing authority.
