⚠️ SCREENSHOTS ARE PLACEHOLDER MOCKUPS, NOT REAL APP CAPTURES
The 4 images in 04_Phone_Screenshots are illustrative mockups (different
logo treatment, a list design and an onboarding screen that don't exist
in the actual app). Google Play requires screenshots that accurately
represent the production app. Before submitting:
- Install the production build on a device/emulator and capture real
  screenshots of the home screen, the scan library, and the ID layout
  chooser (or whichever 2-8 screens best represent the app).
- Do NOT upload the current screenshot 4 (onboarding) — that screen
  does not exist in the app.
